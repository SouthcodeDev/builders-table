Motion notes
Chip selectSnappy scale-and-settle on tap — feels decisive, not fiddly. No drag.
Persona revealLines arrive one at a time, unhurried — reads as thinking, not a spinner ending.
Card swipeFollows the finger 1:1, rotates slightly off-axis, snaps past threshold — never floaty.
Invite arrivalDrops in fast and firm, no bounce — has to read instantly from across a room.
Maps handoffScreen holds a beat in silence before the OS transition — a goodbye, not a redirect.
